    05Common questions

    Good to know

    Can't find your answer? Ask us on WhatsApp.

    How long does an order take?

    Orders take 3 to 7 working days, depending on the quantity and design.

    Is there a minimum order?

    No. There is no minimum order.

    How much does it cost?

    Pricing varies depending on the product, design and quantity of your order. Send your order on WhatsApp and we will reply with a quote.

    What file should I send for my own design?

    PNG, JPG or PDF up to 10 MB. A large, sharp file (5000x5000 pixels) with a plain or transparent background prints best.

    Can you print names or numbers on each shirt?

    Yes. Put the names or numbers in the notes when you build your order.

    Do you deliver or must I collect?

    We cater for both. You can collect your order or have it delivered.

    How do I pay?

    You pay by EFT (electronic funds transfer).
