# Titles and Thumbnails: All Four Videos

Two title options and one thumbnail concept per video. Titles stay under about 60 characters. Thumbnail text is 4 words or fewer so it reads at phone size.

---

## Notes

- **Janitor facts:** the Ronald Read figures ($8 million, the safety pin coat) come from news coverage as remembered. Verify them before publishing with the "Died Rich" or "$8 Million" claims, since the title and thumbnail carry the exact number.
- **Composite stories:** videos 2, 3 and 4 are composite stories, so the titles avoid claiming they're real.
- **Strongest titles:** "A Janitor Left $8 Million" (#1) and "She Said 'I'm Fine' 214 Times" (#3). Both open on a specific number or unlikely contrast.
