She never snapped at the friend
who laughed at her
in front of 12 people.

She just stopped replying,
and 8 months later
she still can't say why.


It was a birthday dinner,
and her friend joked about
her failed job interview.

Everyone laughed,
so she laughed too,
gripping her fork under the table.

That night she typed three replies,
deleted all three,
and put her phone face-down.


She wasn't ignoring her friend.

She was ignoring herself.


In week one,
she muted the group chat
and told herself it was fine.

By month two,
she'd stopped answering anyone
who made her feel small.

By month five,
she was saying "I don't care"
about things she cared about.

By month eight,
40 texts sat unread
and she felt nothing.


Silence isn't peace.
It's an unsent message
that gets heavier every week.

Next time, say it small:
"That one hurt me."
Then breathe.


I'm Dimples.
I talk about the things we feel
but never say out loud.
Follow for more.
