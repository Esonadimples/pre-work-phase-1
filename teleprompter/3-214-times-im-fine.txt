She said "I'm fine"
214 times in one year.

Her sister kept count
on a yellow sticky note.


The tally started the week
she got passed over for a promotion.

Each "I'm fine" came with
the same smile
and the same quick subject change.

The sticky note sat on the fridge,
and she never once looked at it.


Her sister wasn't counting lies.

She was counting every chance
to ask again.


At tally 20,
her sister asked once,
then dropped it.

At tally 80,
she stopped asking
in front of the family.

At tally 150,
she wrote a letter
and never sent it.

At tally 214,
she pulled the note off the fridge
and put it on the table.


"I'm fine" is a door you close politely,
and people stop knocking.

Try this instead:
"I'm not okay yet,
but I'm working on it."


I'm Dimples.
I talk about the things we feel
but never say out loud.
Follow for more.
