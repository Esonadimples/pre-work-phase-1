Being the nicest person on the team
cost him the promotion.

His boss explained why
in one sentence.


He said yes to every request,
even at 9 p.m. on a Friday.

His laptop sat open on the couch
while his dinner went cold.

Coworkers called him "reliable,"
but nobody ever asked
what he wanted.


His boss said,
"I can't promote someone
I've never heard disagree."


In year one,
he took on 6 extra projects
without asking for credit.

In year two,
a new hire with louder opinions
got his best client.

In year three,
he trained the person
who then became his manager.

In year four,
he said "no" once,
and his boss finally leaned in.


Agreeing with everyone
makes you easy to like
and easy to overlook.

Try this once this week:
"I see it differently.
Here's why."


I'm Dimples.
I talk about the things we feel
but never say out loud.
Follow for more.
