## Notes

- **Why they're split:** video 1 is a money story and videos 2-4 are emotional or relationship stories, so they don't share a tag set. Using one set for all four would blur each video's audience.
- **Signature tag:** you can add a branded tag like `#thingswefeel` at the end of every video. It builds a searchable collection across your channel over time.
- **Check before posting:** hashtag popularity shifts, and some tags (for example, mental-health ones) are crowded or flagged by platforms. Try each in the app's search first to see how it looks.
